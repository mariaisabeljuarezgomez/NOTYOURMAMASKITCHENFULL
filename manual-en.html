PART I · CHAPTER 5

05The 5-Minute Quick Start

Follow these steps to make your first update in under five minutes.

  1. Unlock. Click 🔒 Layout Locked in the top bar to switch to 🔓 Unlocked mode — this lets you click and select elements.
  2. Edit text. Double-click any price or item name on the canvas. A cursor appears — type your new values, then click the background to finish.
  3. Move elements. Single-click any element, then drag it to a new position. Use the ARRANGE tab for centering.
  4. Upload a logo. Click the 🛠️ FAB (bottom left) → Upload Img. Select your file — it appears on the canvas automatically.
  5. Save. Click 💾 Save in the top bar. Your changes are now permanent and stored on the server.
  6. Export. Click 🛠️ FAB → ⬇️ Export Pro PNG. Your print-ready menu downloads immediately.
⚠️
Pro tip

Keep the layout Locked unless you're specifically moving elements. In Locked mode you can still edit text by double-clicking — but you won't accidentally drag elements out of place.

PART I · CHAPTER 6

06The Top Header Bar

Always visible at the top of your screen, the header bar provides the core safety and management controls for your session.

🔒 Layout Lock / 🔓 Layout Unlocked

This is the most important control in the editor. By default, the layout is Locked — in this state you cannot drag elements or accidentally click small objects. Think of it as Safe Mode. Switch to Unlocked only when you need to reposition things or use the selection tabs.

💾 Save

Saves your entire session — all text content, positions, styles, layer settings, and image placements — to the server, and creates a local browser backup at the same time. A green toast confirms: "Project Saved Locally." Use Save frequently. Auto-backup also runs automatically every 5 minutes whenever unsaved changes exist. The Save button turns red when changes are pending, and auto-backup shows a brief "Auto-backup saved" notification when it fires.

💡
Shortcut

Save runs automatically every time you click Export Pro PNG, so your export always matches your most recent work.

↺ Undo Last Change

Reverses your most recent action one step at a time. The undo system remembers up to 50 actions — text edits, element moves, style changes, additions, deletions, visibility changes, and lock toggles. Click repeatedly to step back through history.

⛔
Important

Undo does not reverse a Reset to Original or a Load Session. Once you reload or reset, the history is cleared.

🏠 Home

Returns you to the application home dashboard. Always save before clicking Home.

↺ RELOAD

Refreshes the current application. Use this if the editor feels slow or an action becomes unresponsive — it's more reliable than the browser's own refresh button because it maintains the current editor context.

PART I · CHAPTER 8

08The ADD Tab

This tab holds the buttons for adding new content to your menu and performing global file operations.

＋ Add Text

Creates a new text element in the center of your current view, labeled "New Text" and already selected. Type your content immediately and use the Selection Bar to style it.

⬜ Add Rect

Creates a new rectangle shape at the center of the canvas. Use the DESIGN tab to change fill color, corner roundness, border width, and border color — ideal for price backgrounds, banners, and dividers.

Add Circle

Adds a filled circle shape, fully editable for fill color, stroke, opacity, rotation, and size via the DESIGN tab.

Add Star

Adds a five-point star rendered as an SVG polygon — fully styleable with fill color and stroke.

Add Line

Adds a straight, horizontal line. Set weight (0.25–4px), cap style (Flat, Round, Square), and color in DESIGN. Lines can be rotated.

🖼️ Upload Img

Opens your device's file picker. Hold Ctrl (Windows) or Cmd (Mac) to select several images at once — all are imported into your Asset Tray and saved to your permanent library. Transparent PNG borders are auto-trimmed.

👁️ Toggle Original BG

Fades out the background image, showing only your elements on a dark grid — helpful for checking small text or alignment. This setting is saved with your session.

🖼️ Replace Background

If you select an image element on the canvas, the drawer shows a Replace Background button. Clicking it promotes that image to become the new, locked master background for the menu.

🗑️
Remove Background

Found in the Layers panel context. This permanently deletes all background layers without replacing them — use it when you want a transparent or blank canvas behind your content. It is different from Replace Background, which imports something new.

⬇️ Export Pro PNG & 📄 Export Print PDF

Export Pro PNG generates a high-resolution PNG (300 DPI, 12×18 in). It auto-saves first, then renders every visible element and downloads the file — ideal for digital displays and quick previews.

Export Print PDF generates a professional, print-ready PDF using the jsPDF 2.5.1 library, with a 4× scaling engine (300 DPI) for maximum crispness in professional physical printing. It handles complex text, shapes, and high-resolution images with full CORS security support.

🔄 Reset to Original

Clears all your custom edits and restores the menu to the original template delivered by Maria's Digital Designs. Use with caution — this cannot be undone.

PART II · CHAPTER 18

18The DESIGN Tab — Deep Dive

Controls the visual aesthetics of elements — where you set the mood of your menu.

Fonts and typography (text only)

The font menu is populated with high-end typography curated for restaurant use: Bernard MT Condensed, Century Gothic (Bold / Regular / Italic), and other clean, legible options.

Color management

The color picker accepts hex codes or visual selection. Each menu has a designated brand palette — try to stay within it for a cohesive look.

Text spacing

  • Line height — increases the vertical gap between lines in descriptions.
  • Letter spacing — increase for headers to create an elegant, airy look.

Shape design (rectangle only)

  • Fill — the block's primary color.
  • Border — set color and thickness (0–20px).
  • Rounding — turn a square into a pill or a circle by increasing this value.
Design best practice

Use larger, bold fonts for item titles ("GRILLED SALMON") and smaller, regular fonts for descriptions ("Served with lemon caper sauce…").

Contrast rule

Dark background → light text. Light background → dark text. High contrast keeps menus legible in low-light restaurant settings.

PART III · CHAPTER 22

22Save, Load & Auto-Save — Deep Dive

Data safety is the foundation of Menu Editor Pro V2. Here's how the multi-layer saving system works.

Server save — the primary save

Clicking 💾 Save sends the entire state of your menu (the docV2 JSON object) to the PostgreSQL database on Railway. This is the authoritative source of truth — once a server save succeeds, your data is safe even if you switch browsers or computers.

Auto-save protection

If you leave the editor before clicking Save, the system maintains a local browser backup in localStorage. When you return, it checks whether your local version is newer than the server version, and prompts you to restore your unsaved changes if so.

Validation and security

Every save runs a full validation check first, ensuring no invalid elements are added and the file size stays within the 5MB performance limit. If a save fails, the editor highlights the invalid areas and explains how to fix them.

⚠️
Pro tip

After a session, always click 💾 Save and wait for the "Project Saved Locally" toast before closing the tab. Never rely purely on auto-save when switching to another device.

PART III · CHAPTER 23

23Undo History

The ↺ Undo Last Change button — or Ctrl+Z — steps back through your editing history one action at a time, tracking up to 50 actions.

What undo covers
  • Text content edits
  • Element movement and resizing
  • Design changes (fonts, colors, spacing)
  • Adding or deleting elements
  • Visibility / locking / opacity changes
  • Rotation adjustments
What undo doesn't cover
  • Save / Load — you cannot undo a Load Session back to its previous state.
  • Reset to Original — resetting the template is permanent and cannot be reversed by Undo.
PART III · CHAPTER 24

24Export Pro PNG — Complete Reference

Generating the final menu image is a three-step process, handled automatically by the editor's internal rendering engine.

The export pipeline

  1. State capture — the editor saves your current project first, so the output matches your last edit.
  2. High-fidelity render — a hidden, high-resolution canvas loads the master background (3600px wide) and draws every element at 300% zoom for maximum crispness.
  3. CRC32 checksumming — the PNG is assembled with specific metadata chunks so it's recognized as a 300 DPI professional file by printers and digital signs.
Output file format
PNG (lossless)
Color mode
sRGB, 8-bit color
Resolution (DPI)
300 DPI (high resolution)
Total resolution (pixels)
3600w × 5400h

Export dimensions: 3600 × 5400 pixels at 300 DPI (12 × 18 inches print-ready). The exported file is named notyourmamaskitchen-menu.png.

Common export questions

Is the export different on mobile? No — the export engine is server-synced and uses the same high-resolution logic regardless of the device that triggers it.

Can I export a transparent menu? Only if you've removed the background layer entirely in the Layers panel. Otherwise the menu includes the background frame.

⏳
Wait for the finish

A progress bar appears during export. Do not click anything until the file download actually begins — on slow connections, rendering the high-res frame can take up to 20 seconds.

PART IV · CHAPTER 26

26Zoom & Canvas Navigation

Managing your view is critical on both laptop and mobile. The editor includes a high-performance viewport controller.

Zoom controls

Controls are located at the bottom-right of the screen:

  • Fit to Screen — snaps the canvas to fit perfectly within your current window, the default view.
  • ＋ Zoom In — magnifies the canvas, required for reading and editing small font sizes (10–14px) and precise placement.
  • － Zoom Out — shrinks the view, useful for seeing the whole menu at once.

Zoom memory: zoom resets to fit-to-screen every time the page reloads — use the zoom controls to re-adjust after reload.

Pinch zoom on mobile

On touchscreens, use a two-finger pinch to zoom in and out. Pinch zoom only works when the layout is Unlocked, and multi-touch is automatically suppressed to prevent accidental drag while pinching.

Canvas panning (scrolling)

When the layout is Locked, you can freely scroll the canvas by dragging anywhere on the background — your finger or mouse moves the view without affecting any elements. This is the safest way to navigate a zoomed canvas.

💡
Best practice

Zoom in to work on fine detail — small text, precise alignment — then zoom back out to see the full composition before exporting.

Rulers and guides

A horizontal ruler runs across the top and a vertical ruler down the left of the canvas. Drag from either ruler onto the canvas to place a guide line — a non-printing alignment reference.

  • Place a guide — drag from the top ruler (horizontal) or left ruler (vertical) onto the canvas.
  • Move a guide — click and drag the guide line to reposition it.
  • Delete a guide — hover to reveal the ✕ button, then click it.

Rulers and guides are hidden on mobile devices.

PART IV · CHAPTER 28

28Reset to Original

The 🔄 Reset button in the Tools Drawer restores the menu to its original template — exactly as it was when first delivered to you.

⛔
Cannot be undone

Reset to Original cannot be undone with the ↺ Undo button. Once confirmed, all your edits are permanently cleared. Only use Reset when you intentionally want to start fresh.

Reset confirmation

Clicking Reset opens a confirmation dialog before anything is changed. Read it carefully before confirming — click Cancel if you clicked Reset by accident.

When to use Reset

  • You want to completely start over with a clean template.
  • Your layout has become too cluttered or confused to repair through editing.
  • You're starting a new seasonal menu version from scratch.

In all other cases, use ↺ Undo for individual mistakes and 📂 Load Session to return to your last saved state.

PART IV · CHAPTER 29

29Cross-Device Syncing

Menu Editor Pro V2 uses server-side persistence — your saved sessions live securely on the server, not just in your browser — so you can work on any device and always reach your latest saved version.

How to continue on another device

  1. On your current device, click 💾 Save Session to push all current work to the server.
  2. Open your editor link on the second device (phone, tablet, or another computer).
  3. Open the drawer and click 📂 Load Session.
  4. Your complete menu — all text, images, styles, and positions — loads exactly as saved.
💡
Tip

When you open the editor on a new device for the first time, it automatically loads your most recent local browser backup if one exists. Still, always click Load Session to confirm you have the latest server version.

What gets synced

  • All text content and styles
  • All element positions and sizes
  • All layer settings (visibility, lock state, opacity, role)
  • All image placements and positions
  • Your uploaded image library, stored in the Asset Tray

30Mobile Guide — iPhone, Android & Tablet

I can't click on an element

Most likely cause: the layout is Locked — click 🔒 Layout Locked to switch to 🔓 Unlocked, then try again.

Second cause: the element's Lock toggle is on in the LAYER tab. Open the Layers Panel, find its row, and click its 🔒 icon to unlock it.

I accidentally moved an element to the wrong position

Click ↺ Undo Last Change (or press Ctrl+Z) immediately — the element returns to its previous position. The system tracks up to 50 steps, so you can step back through several accidental changes.

My text edits aren't showing up

Make sure you double-clicked the text element to enter editing mode (you should see a blinking cursor) — a single click only selects it. Also confirm you clicked the canvas background afterward, since text edits are only committed when you exit the element.

The exported PNG looks blurry or low quality

The file is always generated at full 300 DPI regardless of how it looks on screen. Large PNG files at 300 DPI appear small on screen because your monitor has fewer pixels per inch than the file — open it in a photo viewer at 100% zoom, or send it directly to your printer where it prints crisply.

A hidden element is appearing in my export

Check the element's visibility in the Layers Panel. The 👁️ icon must show as 🙈 (hidden) for the element to be excluded — if it shows 👁️, the element is visible and will appear.

The editor is running slowly or appears frozen

Save your work first, then click ↺ RELOAD in the header — unsaved changes will be lost. After reloading, use Load Session to restore your last saved state.

I lost my changes after closing the browser

Changes are only permanently saved when you click 💾 Save. The editor keeps a local browser backup, but this can be cleared by browser updates or cache cleaning. Best habit: save after every significant edit.

Save or Load is failing or hanging

Usually a temporary internet connection issue. Check your connection — the editor retries automatically. If it persists, try RELOAD, then attempt Save again. The local browser backup protects your most recent work during connection interruptions.

An uploaded image isn't appearing on the canvas

Check the Layers Panel — the image may be placed but hidden (🙈) or at 0% opacity. Confirm the file format is supported: JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, or SVG. Very large files may take a moment to process.

I can't find the Selection Bar — it disappeared

It only appears when an element is selected — click or tap any element to bring it back. If you previously dragged it off-screen, reload the page (save first) to reset the toolbar position.

32Best Practices

These habits protect your work, keep your menu looking professional, and make editing fast and frustration-free.

Saving habits

  • Save after every section of work. Update a group of prices → Save. Add a new item description → Save. Move an image into position → Save.
  • Save before closing the browser — even if you're "just stepping away for a minute."
  • Save before switching devices — server-side save is the bridge between your phone and your computer.
  • The 💾 Save button lives in the top header bar, always one tap away.

Layout Lock habits

  • Keep Layout Locked by default. Only unlock when you need to drag something — re-lock immediately after.
  • On mobile especially, Locked mode prevents dozens of potential accidental repositioning errors.
  • Text editing (double-click to type) works in both Locked and Unlocked mode — you never need to unlock just to edit text.

Typography best practices

  • Keep section headers larger (28–36px) and item names smaller (16–22px) for clear visual hierarchy.
  • Use center alignment for titles, prices, and featured items; left alignment for descriptions and lists.
  • Limit your menu to 2–3 font choices maximum for a clean, professional look.
  • Use positive letter spacing on headers for an elegant, premium appearance.
  • Increase line height (1.4–1.6) for paragraph descriptions to improve readability.

Image best practices

  • Use PNG with transparency for logos — the editor auto-trims the transparent edges.
  • Upload images at the highest resolution you have — the editor handles large files well.
  • Use the Opacity slider in LAYER to fade decorative images that shouldn't compete with text.
  • Lock any image that should never move (background frame, watermark) with the 🔒 toggle.

Layer organization

  • Give elements meaningful names in LAYER ("main-title", "price-col-1", "logo") for easy identification in the Layers Panel.
  • Use the Layers Panel to select hidden or stacked elements instead of clicking around on the canvas.
  • Set background frames and decorative borders to Role: Background + Locked so they can never be accidentally moved.

Before exporting

  • Review all text for spelling errors — zoom in on each section.
  • Check that all prices are correctly updated.
  • Verify nothing you want visible is accidentally hidden (check the Layers Panel).
  • Save your session — export auto-saves, but it's good habit to save manually first.
  • Export, then open the file and zoom to 100% to inspect quality before sending to print.

Generating an image

  1. Expand 🖼️ Generate Image.
  2. Enter a prompt describing what you want (e.g. "a beautifully plated grilled salmon with lemon and herbs, restaurant lighting").
  3. Optionally click ✨ Enhance Prompt to let AI improve your description.
  4. Choose an Aspect Ratio and Style.
  5. Optionally enter a Negative Prompt to exclude unwanted elements.
  6. Click Generate Image — it appears in the panel as a preview.
  7. Click Upload to Cloudinary to host it, or Place on Canvas to add it directly to your menu.

Generating a video

  1. Expand 🎬 Generate Video.
  2. Optionally upload a reference image to guide the video's starting frame.
  3. Enter a prompt describing the motion (e.g. "steam rising from a bowl of ramen, slow cinematic pull-back").
  4. Use ✨ Enhance Prompt for better results.
  5. Configure Duration, Resolution, and Camera Motion.
  6. Adjust Creativity vs. Prompt Fidelity (CFG Scale) — lower is more creative, higher follows the prompt more strictly.
  7. Click Generate Video — it's submitted to Kling AI asynchronously, with a progress indicator.
  8. Once ready, the video plays in the panel. Click Send to Viewer to auto-populate the matching Viewer Panel URL field for your chosen resolution.

The Enhance Prompt feature

Sends your text to the backend, where it's augmented with professional food photography modifiers. For images it adds terms like professional food photography, soft natural lighting, shallow depth of field, 85mm lens; for videos, cinematic food video, slow motion, professional lighting, 4K quality. You can edit the result before generating.

How AI images are placed on the canvas

Clicking Place on Canvas first uploads the image to your Cloudinary CDN if it isn't already there. A new image element is created at the center of your viewport, sized 300×300px — move, resize, and style it like any other element, using the standard V2 image element schema.

PART VI · CHAPTER 36

36Export Print PDF (300 DPI) — Deep Dive

While the standard PNG export is excellent for digital displays, Export Print PDF is engineered specifically for professional physical printing — a high-resolution pipeline that keeps your menu just as sharp on paper as it is on screen.

High-resolution pipeline

  • 4× scaling — the engine renders the entire canvas at 4 times base size, equivalent to 300 DPI at 12×18 inches.
  • Full composite — every element (high-res customer images, vector-like shapes, anti-aliased text) is composited onto a high-bandwidth offscreen canvas before PDF generation.
  • CORS compliance — images are fetched via specialized proxy headers so the export never hangs or fails due to security restrictions.

How to export

  1. Make sure your layout is exactly as you want it.
  2. Click the 🛠️ Tools Drawer and select ⬇️ Export Print PDF.
  3. Wait for the "Preparing print-ready PDF" toast to complete — high-resolution rendering may take 2–5 seconds.
  4. The menu-export.pdf file downloads automatically.
📦
Large file size

Because these PDFs are optimized for print quality, they can be significantly larger than regular PNGs. Ensure you have a stable connection when exporting.
